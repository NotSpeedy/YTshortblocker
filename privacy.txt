Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

Overview

This browser extension is designed to block YouTube Shorts and improve your browsing experience. Your privacy is extremely important to us.

Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

No personal information is collected
No browsing history is stored externally
No analytics, tracking, or telemetry is used
Local Storage

The extension may store minimal data locally on your device to function properly.

All data is stored only within your browser
No data is sent to external servers
Data never leaves your device
Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

Clearing your browser data will remove all stored extension data
Uninstalling the extension will delete all associated data
No backups or external copies of data exist
Third-Party Services

This extension:

Does not use third-party services
Does not integrate with external APIs
Does not include advertisements or trackers
Account Requirement

No account, signup, or login is required to use this extension.

Security

Because no data is transmitted or stored externally, your information remains fully under your control on your local device.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, updates will be reflected in this document with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
